Our Story

From a Single Vision to a Thriving Conglomerate

SH Group was founded in 2020 in Hyderabad with a single, powerful vision — to build something meaningful that impacts lives. What began as SH Traders, a building materials supply business providing premium cement, steel, and construction essentials, quickly grew into something much larger.

Recognizing the immense potential in the construction industry, we expanded into SH Builders — offering end-to-end house construction, stunning elevation designs, and real estate services. Our journey didn't stop there. With a passion for healthcare innovation, we launched SH Medical Devices, bringing high-quality surgical instruments and medical equipment to hospitals and clinics across India.

Today, SH Group stands as a diversified conglomerate — trusted by 200+ clients, with 20+ successful projects, and three thriving business divisions. Our story is one of ambition, integrity, and relentless pursuit of excellence.
